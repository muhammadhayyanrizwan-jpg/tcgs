Cookie Policy

The City Grammar School, Karachi

Document Status: Draft, to be reviewed by the school administration.

This Cookie Policy explains how The City Grammar School (TCGS) utilizes cookies and local browser storage to provide a reliable web experience.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies and browser session storage are small text fragments retained temporarily in your browser to remember navigation preferences, such as pre-filling student result queries during active sessions.

2. Essential & Functional Usage

Our website only employs functional and session-based storage (such as sessionStorage) to enable features like quick result lookups and language preferences. We do not use intrusive third-party advertising cookies.

3. Managing Storage Preferences

You can adjust your browser settings at any time to block or erase local storage entries without impacting the core readability of school information.
