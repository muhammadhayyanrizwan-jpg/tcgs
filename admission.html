Curriculum Breakdown

Classes Offered: Primary to Matric

Select a grade level below to explore focus areas and learning outcomes.

Foundation Stage

Primary School: Developing Curiosity & Character

In our Primary division, students build strong academic fundamentals in English, Urdu, Mathematics, and General Science while being nurtured in a warm and positive environment.

  • Phonics, creative writing, and bilingual comprehension
  • Mental math and introductory coding puzzles
  • Art, moral values, and cooperative teamwork

Key Learning Outcomes

Students transition into confident readers and logical thinkers, developing daily self-discipline and an eagerness to explore new ideas.

Exploration Stage

Middle School: Analytical Thinking & Hands-on STEM

Middle schoolers transition into deeper subject specialization. Science experiments in physical laboratories and robotics workshops become part of their weekly routine.

  • Hands-on STEM practical experiments in laboratory settings
  • Robotics & AI fundamentals and app logic development
  • Public speaking presentations, declamation, and debates

Key Learning Outcomes

Mastery of analytical problem solving and scientific reasoning, preparing every student thoroughly for secondary board examinations.

Board Specialization

Matric (SSC): Board Excellence & Practical Mastery

Affiliated with the Board of Secondary Education Karachi (BSEK), our 9th and 10th grade programs combine thorough syllabus mastery with rigorous practical examinations.

  • Science (Biology / Computer Science) & General Faculty
  • Frequent mock board exams and personalized conceptual clinics
  • Complete laboratory practical journals and viva voce training

Key Learning Outcomes

High-percentile BSEK Matric board grades enabling seamless entry into top colleges, coupled with enduring ethical values.
