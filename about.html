Institutional Overview

Navigating the Journey of Intellectual Growth

The City Grammar School, located on Jail Road, Karachi, offers quality education from Primary to Matric. We combine a strong academic foundation with modern learning methods, robotics, coding, STEM and public speaking, so students grow into confident, disciplined and curious learners.

Our experienced teachers give personal attention in a safe and supportive environment, preparing students not only for board exams but for life.

Progressive Milestones

Milestones of a Student's Journey

How every young learner advances from early foundational curiosity to Matric board distinction.

Phase 1 • Primary (Grades 1–5)

Building Core Literacy & Curiosity

Students develop foundational language fluency, mental arithmetic, artistic creativity, and social collaboration in a joyful and structured classroom setting.

Phase 2 • Middle School (Grades 6–8)

Hands-On STEM & Practical Inquiry

Students enter experimental science labs, embark on coding & app development, build robotics models, and hone speech presentation confidence on stage.

Phase 3 • Matric (Grades 9–10 SSC)

Rigorous Academic Mastery & Board Success

Focused BSEK Karachi board preparation guided by veteran faculty, comprehensive mock exams, dedicated laboratory practicals, and ethical career counseling.
